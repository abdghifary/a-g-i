Agi worked at Amar Bank from December 2019 to December 2025, a total of six years. He served as Senior Frontend Engineer, architecting and delivering scalable web applications for B2B and B2C financial products. He owned features end-to-end from requirements gathering through deployment and impact measurement.

Established automated quality gates including ESLint, SonarQube, and pre-commit hooks. Guided a QA engineer transitioning to frontend development, who now works as a full-time frontend developer.
