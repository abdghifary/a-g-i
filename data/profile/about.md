# About

## Background

Agi is a self-taught software engineer with six years of production experience, currently based in Bogor, Indonesia. He holds a B.Sc. in Agricultural Industrial Technology from Padjadjaran University, not a computer science degree. His path into software development began with his undergraduate thesis, when his professor assigned him to build a web-based inventory management system for a local small business. That project led him to frontend development, where he progressed from junior to senior engineer.

At Amar Bank, Agi spent six years architecting and delivering scalable web applications for B2B and B2C financial products. He advanced from junior to senior frontend engineer while building a component library adopted by four squads, reducing feature development time by 40%, and establishing CI/CD and code review standards for the entire frontend organization.

## Work Style

Agi approaches engineering with a focus on scalability, maintainability, and long-term impact. When he built Amar Bank's component library, he planned adoption paths, documentation, and versioning strategy rather than delivering isolated components. He works across frontend and backend boundaries, having designed API contracts with backend teams, understood database constraints affecting frontend performance, and optimized queries.

He takes ownership of production systems. At Amar Bank, he identified and resolved a video upload bug causing 40% error rates, tracing the root cause to a configuration typo and restoring service in 45 minutes. He mentors engineers across levels, including a QA engineer who transitioned to frontend development under his guidance and now works as a full-time frontend developer.

Under pressure, Agi clarifies scope, prioritizes features by user impact and technical risk, and communicates delays early with proposed solutions rather than surprises. He assumes positive intent in disagreements, seeking to understand others' positions fully before framing conflicts around shared goals.
